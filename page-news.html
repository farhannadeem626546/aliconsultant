A CLOSER LOOK · KEEPING INFORMED

Understand an update before acting on it.

01

Check the original source

Look for the institution or official authority behind an announcement. Verify when it was published and which applicants it applies to.

02

Identify what changes for you

Separate a general announcement from the requirements of your particular programme or route. Ask questions when the practical effect is unclear.

03

Update your plan carefully

Record any confirmed change to your application timeline or preparation. Contact the relevant institution or authority for case-specific answers.

GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEARER ANSWERS

Before your next step.

Ask your question ↗
What should I do first?+

Start with the checklist above for keeping informed. Gather the information you already have, note what is missing and choose one practical task to complete next.

Can I begin if I have not chosen a destination?+

Yes. Start with your subject interests, qualification, study level and practical budget. Counselling can help you structure the research you need before choosing.

Where do I confirm current requirements?+

Use the intended institution’s official programme and admission information, and the relevant official authority for visa or recognition requirements. Ask for clarification if instructions are unclear.

How do I discuss my own circumstances?+

Open the Free Counselling form or visit Contact. The enquiry prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Include your qualification, goal and the question you want to discuss.
