Your study journey

From “where do I start?”
to “I’m ready to go.”

Explore each stage to understand how we help you move forward.

STEP 01 / 08

Start with your ambitions

Tell us about your education, your preferred subject and what you hope to achieve. A first conversation helps turn a broad idea into a useful starting point.

STEP 02 / 08

Understand your options

We review your academic background, English preparation, budget and timing to identify questions that need answering before you apply.

STEP 03 / 08

Find your academic fit

Compare course content, eligibility, campus location, tuition and living costs. A thoughtful shortlist makes the next decisions easier.

STEP 04 / 08

Prepare a clear application

Organise the requested documents, check each institution’s process and submit the information it needs to assess your application.

STEP 05 / 08

Review the next steps

Read any offer carefully. Understand academic and English conditions, deposits and deadlines before deciding how to proceed.

STEP 06 / 08

Prepare your documentation

Work through the relevant official requirements and organise your evidence. We support preparation; immigration authorities make the final decision.

STEP 07 / 08

Get ready for your arrival

Plan accommodation, travel, key documents and the first tasks you will need to complete when you arrive.

STEP 08 / 08

Settle into your next chapter

Stay connected for guidance as you begin your studies, get to know your campus and adjust to your new environment.

How do I start my study abroad journey?

Book a free counselling conversation. Share your qualifications, study interests, preferred destination and budget so we can discuss suitable next steps.

Can you help me choose a country and university?

Yes. We help compare course options, entry conditions, location, costs and timing around your academic profile and goals.

Do you help with scholarship applications?

We can help you research funding options and understand eligibility. Availability, award values and deadlines depend on the provider.

What if I have not taken an English test yet?

We can discuss the English evidence your intended course requires and preparation options through ELC.

Do you provide visa application assistance?

We support documentation and preparation for the relevant application process. Visa decisions remain with the immigration authority.

How does the counselling form work?

It prepares a WhatsApp message with your details. You review and send it directly to Ali Consultants.

Ask your question ↗
What should I do first?+

Start with the checklist above for your study abroad journey. Gather the information you already have, note what is missing and choose one practical task to complete next.

Can I begin if I have not chosen a destination?+

Yes. Start with your subject interests, qualification, study level and practical budget. Counselling can help you structure the research you need before choosing.

Where do I confirm current requirements?+

Use the intended institution’s official programme and admission information, and the relevant official authority for visa or recognition requirements. Ask for clarification if instructions are unclear.

How do I discuss my own circumstances?+

Open the Free Counselling form or visit Contact. The enquiry prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Include your qualification, goal and the question you want to discuss.
