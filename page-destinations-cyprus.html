Build your plan

Your Cyprus application,
step by step.

A clear plan connects your academic choices with the practical requirements of studying abroad.

01

Academic eligibility

Compare your qualifications and results with the published entry criteria for each programme. Some courses request additional evidence or experience.

02

English evidence

Check the tests and scores accepted by your chosen institution. Plan time for preparation, results and any follow-up requirements.

03

Application documents

Prepare transcripts, certificates, identification and any course-specific statements or references requested by the university.

04

Funding and costs

Look at tuition alongside accommodation, transport, living costs, insurance and application expenses before deciding.

Before you apply

Bring your essentials together.

The exact documents depend on the course, institution and application route. This is a practical starting checklist.

Know what to
ask next.

Your circumstances matter. We can help you research the details for your intended course.

Which course should I choose?

Start with your academic background, interests and career direction. Compare course modules and entry criteria before creating a shortlist.

How much should I budget?

Build an individual estimate for tuition, accommodation, living costs, travel, insurance and application expenses. Costs vary by city, programme and personal circumstances.

Can I apply for a scholarship?

Eligibility depends on the award. Check academic, nationality, degree-level and course criteria with the provider before applying.

What is the next step?

Share your qualification, subject interest and preferred intake with our team. We can discuss what to research and prepare next.
