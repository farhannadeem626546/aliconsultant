Student guide

How to plan a study abroad application.

Begin with your goals, qualifications, budget and preferred intake. Compare official course requirements, prepare documents early and leave room for decisions and visa processing.

✦   Explore your next step
A practical starting point

What to do next

Start by writing down why you want to study abroad, the subject you want to pursue and the qualification you already have.

Research entry criteria on university course pages, including grades, English evidence and any portfolio or experience requirements.

Estimate tuition and living costs together.

Work backwards from official deadlines so you have time to obtain documents, submit applications and review an offer carefully.

Requirements and deadlines change. Use official university and government sources for current details.
