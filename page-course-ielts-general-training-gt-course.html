A CLOSER LOOK · IELTS GENERAL TRAINING

Prepare for IELTS General Training with a clear routine.

01

Everyday communication and structured responses

Work on reading practical texts, expressing your ideas and communicating clearly. Confirm which test type is required for your intended purpose before booking.

02

Practice with a purpose

Break preparation into manageable sessions. Review your mistakes, repeat difficult tasks and practise communicating clearly instead of only counting completed exercises.

03

Discuss your preparation route

Tell ELC about your current level, goal and intended timeline. Ask about current class availability, teaching format and the preparation approach before enrolling.

Before your next step.

Ask your question ↗
What should I do first?+

Start with the checklist above for ielts general training. Gather the information you already have, note what is missing and choose one practical task to complete next.

Can I begin if I have not chosen a destination?+

Yes. Start with your subject interests, qualification, study level and practical budget. Counselling can help you structure the research you need before choosing.

Where do I confirm current requirements?+

Use the intended institution’s official programme and admission information, and the relevant official authority for visa or recognition requirements. Ask for clarification if instructions are unclear.

How do I discuss my own circumstances?+

Open the Free Counselling form or visit Contact. The enquiry prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Include your qualification, goal and the question you want to discuss.
