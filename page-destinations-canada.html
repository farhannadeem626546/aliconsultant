A CLOSER LOOK · STUDY IN CANADA

Build your Canada plan around your goals.

01

Understand the qualification

Check whether you are applying for a degree, diploma or another qualification. Compare the learning outcomes, entry conditions and institution rather than treating all programmes as interchangeable.

02

Compare the complete cost

Build a budget using the specific programme’s tuition and your own living arrangements. Include travel, insurance, application expenses and a contingency before deciding what is affordable.

03

Give preparation enough time

Record application dates, language preparation and document requirements together. Keep unanswered questions in a list to discuss with your counsellor or the institution.

What should I do first?+

Start with the checklist above for study in canada. Gather the information you already have, note what is missing and choose one practical task to complete next.

Can I begin if I have not chosen a destination?+

Yes. Start with your subject interests, qualification, study level and practical budget. Counselling can help you structure the research you need before choosing.

Where do I confirm current requirements?+

Use the intended institution’s official programme and admission information, and the relevant official authority for visa or recognition requirements. Ask for clarification if instructions are unclear.

How do I discuss my own circumstances?+
